I am a mathematical engineer of Mines Paris and a Ph.D. in computer science.
My doctoral work studies how to merge statistical guarantees and learning methods, with an emphasis on online guarantees and monitoring.

## My Life

Raised in Corsica, I followed the classical path of French pupils interested in maths and did preparatory classes (MPSI-MP*) in the Lycée Saint Louis, in Paris.
I later studied mathematical engineering at the École des Mines de Paris, and completed a master's in machine learning at Paris-Dauphine in parallel to the last year.
I then joined <a href='https://www.dsme.rwth-aachen.de/cms/~ibtrg/dsme/?lidx=1'>Prof. Trimpe's group</a> for my Ph.D., wherefrom I recently graduated.

## Current Work

While my Ph.D. focused mainly on theory and statistics, I am now intensively learning about LLMs and large scale inference.
My goal is to understand how this (now) unavoidable technology works in details, and how my skills in anytime-valid statistics can help improve it; e.g., by monitoring it at deployment or smart benchmarking.

I also enjoy callisthenics, reading, theater, and chess.
